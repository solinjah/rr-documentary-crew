# Channel profile

The idea, title, hook, retention and thumbnail bots read this file. Update it when the numbers change.

## Niche
Twisted history and dark lore: true, documented stories that sound impossible, told by the Archivist, a calm and exact
narrator. Shorts of 60 to 75 seconds and documentary episodes of about 10 minutes ("Extreme Case Files"). Every story
is on the record; legends are labelled as legends.

## Audience
- Mostly older: about 60% are 45 or over and about 25% are 65 or over. Almost no viewers under 25.
- About 68% male.
- They come for history they have never heard, told straight. They dislike hype, shouting, fake facts and obvious AI
  mistakes in pictures.

## What performs
- Best: lesser-known single-person stories with an "impossible, but on record" claim (one person, one strange fact).
- Weaker: famous topics everyone already knows; broad "top 10" lists.
- About 62% of viewers stayed to watch rather than swiping away.
- Viewers drop off when a picture stays on screen longer than about 10 seconds.
